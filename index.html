Enable Motion Sensors

This app needs access to your phone's orientation sensors to measure how far you turn the wheel. Nothing leaves your device.

How it works

  1. Build the wheel. Cut a disc of cardboard and tape your phone flat to the middle, screen facing you.
  2. Hold it straight. Grip the wheel in its neutral, straight-ahead position.
  3. Calibrate. Tap Calibrate Center. That exact orientation becomes 0.
  4. Drive. Turning left gives you −100, right gives +100. Everything is measured relative to the calibrated centre, not the compass.
  5. Tune. Raise sensitivity for twitchier steering, raise dead zone if the value drifts at rest, and set max angle to how far you actually turn the cardboard.
